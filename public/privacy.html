Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • Last updated: September 2026

At SPOT ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy and trust are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how our strength training and fitness application collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data when you use the SPOT mobile app and associated services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide an intelligent, personalized workout experience:

  • Account Credentials: When you choose to sync your workouts across devices, we collect your email address and authentication credentials securely managed through Supabase Auth.
  • Training & Workout Data: Details of your training sessions, including exercises, logged sets, repetitions, weight values, rest intervals, workout routines, and custom exercise templates.
  • Body Metrics (Optional): Height, body weight logs, and preferred units of measurement (kg/lbs, cm/ft) used solely to calculate volume load and training readiness.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and crash logs to maintain app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to power your fitness journey:

  • To generate, calibrate, and adapt workout routines to your personal goals and split preferences.
  • To display training analytics, progressive overload trends, and personal best records (PRs).
  • To securely synchronize your workout history to your cloud account so you never lose your progress.
  • To send local rest-timer and training reminder notifications (only when enabled by you).

3. Data Protection & Security

All communication between the SPOT app and cloud servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption. Your data is stored in secure, access-controlled cloud infrastructure hosted by Supabase with row-level security (RLS) policies ensuring that only you can read or write your personal workout data.

4. No Sale or Third-Party Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or fitness information to data brokers, advertisers, or third-party marketing companies. We do not serve third-party ads in SPOT.

5. Account Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your data at all times in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA):

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete all your stored workout history, custom programs, and cloud profile at any time by going to Profile → Danger Zone → Reset App Data / Delete Account. This permanently purges your records.
  • Manual Deletion Request: You may also request complete removal of your account and all associated cloud data by emailing our support team at support@spotapp.fit. We process all deletion requests within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

SPOT is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates and legal compliance. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
